QUESTION 5 - EXPLANATIONS

This question is theoretical and does not require execution of Linux commands.

The answer evaluates the recovery mechanisms available in vi:
- Swap files
- Undo history
- Registers
- Backup files
- Auto-recovery

It concludes that the combination of swap files and vi's auto-recovery feature is the most reliable strategy because it restores the most recent unsaved changes after a system crash. Backup files provide an additional safeguard by preserving the previous version of the file.
